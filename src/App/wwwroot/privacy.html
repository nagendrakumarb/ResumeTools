Professional Hub / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Professional Hub. The service is designed to perform resume analysis inside your browser, but some optional features contact third-party services.

Resume files and generated documents

PDF and DOCX resume files are read and processed on your device by the Blazor WebAssembly application. Professional Hub does not operate a server that receives or stores those resume files. Uploaded resume text and pasted job descriptions are private tool inputs: they are not published, shared with, or made visible to other Professional Hub users. Corrected documents are generated in the browser and downloaded to your device. If you grant access to a local folder, the browser uses the permission you approved to save application records or documents there.

Local browser storage

The application may store preferences, analysis summaries, job identifiers, and application-tracking records in browser storage such as IndexedDB. Resume text is not intended to be stored as an analytics record. You can remove local information by using application controls where provided, clearing site data in your browser, or removing files from a folder you mapped.

Job searches, imported URLs, and provider configuration

When you search an external job source, your browser sends the selected keywords, locations, filters, and necessary authentication details directly to that provider. When you import a job URL, the browser may request that public URL directly or send the URL to the configured page-reading provider if direct access is blocked. Those providers receive the request and may process IP addresses and technical request information under their own privacy policies.

A provider configuration file is read into the current browser session. Secrets supplied to browser code cannot be treated as server-protected secrets. Do not use a high-value or unrestricted credential.

Advertising and Cookies

Professional Hub uses Google AdSense to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Google and third-party vendors use cookies (such as the Google DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Personal information and sensitive data

Do not paste sensitive information that is unnecessary for resume analysis. Professional Hub does not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, or resume contents to advertising services. The site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Security and limitations

Local processing reduces server-side collection, but no browser application can promise absolute security. Browser extensions, device malware, external providers, and users who share downloaded files are outside Professional Hub's control.

Changes and questions

This policy may change when features or providers change. The effective date will be updated. Questions and issue reports can be submitted through the public Professional Hub repository issue tracker.
