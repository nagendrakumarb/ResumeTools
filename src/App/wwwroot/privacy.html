Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Professional Hub ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using our platform. Our core application is architected to perform client-side resume analysis directly inside your browser, preventing your private employment data from being collected on remote servers.

1. Client-Side Resume Processing

PDF, DOCX, and text resume files uploaded to Professional Hub are parsed locally on your device via Blazor WebAssembly. Professional Hub does not operate backend servers that collect, store, or transmit your uploaded resumes or target job descriptions. Your documents remain strictly inside your browser environment and are never made visible to third parties or other users.

2. Browser Storage & Local Data Retention

To improve user experience, the application may save session preferences, analysis summaries, and job tracking records in local browser storage (such as IndexedDB or LocalStorage). You can permanently clear this data at any time through your browser settings or by clearing site data.

3. External Job Searches & Import Links

When using optional job-search or link-import features, your browser establishes a direct connection with the respective external job board or provider. These third-party services may log technical request metadata (such as IP addresses and browser headers) subject to their own respective privacy policies.

4. Advertising, Cookies, and Third-Party Vendors

Professional Hub uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. AdSense and third-party vendors use cookies—including the DoubleClick cookie—to serve non-personalized or personalized ads based on your visit to this site and other websites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior web visits.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant ads based on browsing history.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

5. Sensitive Information

We advise against pasting sensitive personal identifiers (such as government identification numbers, financial details, or sensitive health records) into resume tools. Professional Hub does not sell, share, or monetize user data.

6. Policy Updates & Inquiries

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any adjustments will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date. Technical questions or issue reports can be raised via our official GitHub Repository Tracker.
